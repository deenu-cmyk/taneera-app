Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel or get a refund on my order?

No — Taneeraa's follows a strict No Refund Policy. Once payment is confirmed, the order is final, so please double-check your order details before completing payment. See our Refund Policy for details.

Can I exchange a product after delivery?

Products cannot normally be exchanged after delivery. If you received a damaged or incorrect item, please contact our support team and we'll review your case.

How long does delivery take?

Orders are usually processed within 1–3 business days, with delivery generally taking 3–7 business days depending on your location. See Shipping Information for more.

How do I track my order?

Head to the Track Your Order section and enter your Order ID (e.g. TAN-10025), or check the Orders icon in the header if you're logged in.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI / Online Payment at checkout.
